# Entity state / pose freezes (pin set)

Load when touching, gating or deleting one of the entity pins, freezes or `*IdleMixin` drivers below
- and read *When to delete a freeze mixin* at the end before removing any. Writing a new one is
[EntityPins.md](EntityPins.md). Indexed from [`harness/CLAUDE.md`](../../harness/CLAUDE.md).

Formulas and the freeze / pin catalog are in [README](../../harness/README.md)'s Mixins section, which names 25 of the 55 classes `refharness.client.mixins.json` registers. Quick reference:

- `FreezeAnimationStateMixin` (`LivingEntityRenderer.extractRenderState`) - zero per-tick anim fields (`deathTime`, `ticksSinceKineticHitFeedback`, `wornHeadAnimationPos`, ...); force `isInWater` on `AbstractFish` (salmon/cod/tropical_fish upright). **Three of its fields are answered by `AnimationClock` rather than pinned**: `ageInTicks` is the frame's tick on a posed run, and `walkAnimationSpeed` / `walkAnimationPos` are, on a walking one, the speed and the tick times the walk-clock rate the subject's schedule armed - vanilla's own render-state values for a subject walking at that pace, its baby scale already inside the rate. **On a posed run the walk pair reaches the subject alone**: arming names the entity the frame draws, `AnimationClock.isSubject(entity)` answers whether a render state is that entity's, and every other entity the client extracts - a world mob in the level behind the capture, drawn before the first frame is armed or while another subject's is - keeps the pair vanilla's own extract gave it. **The capture holds the line from the other side**: `EntityFrameRenderer` refuses to measure or draw an entity on a posed run that no frame is armed for, so a subject armed under another instance, or under none, fails rather than coming out as a stray, and `EntityAnimationScheduleTest` in asset-renderer's suite holds both the refusal and every arm naming the entity it draws. A frozen run zeroes the pair on every entity, and `ageInTicks` reaches every render state alike.
- `SuppressShakingMixin` (`LivingEntityRenderer.setupRotations`) - cancel the `isShaking` bodyRot wobble.
- `SkipSetupAnimMixin` (every `setupAnim` callsite) - authored bind pose, not frame-0 animated pose. **Broadest freeze**, and the one `PoseState.posed()` lifts - which both posed gaits answer true for.
- `WitchNoseMixin` (`WitchRenderer`) - `entityId = IdleFigures.PINNED_ENTITY_ID` (9). Vanilla bobs the nose at `sin(ageInTicks * 0.01 * (entityId % 10))` so a crowd does not bob in lockstep, and an id comes off a counter over every entity the client has built, so the frequency would be a function of run history. Nine because it is the highest of the ten frequencies and shows the most of the cycle in a strip; zero would hold the nose still. Inert while `setupAnim` is frozen; without it the animated set does not reproduce.
- No bee pin. `BeeModel.setupAnim` skips the wing flap and the body bob when `isOnGround`, and a
  never-ticked bee reports false - so forcing the landed pose was a state no subject here is in,
  inert on every frozen sub-tree and worth 120 of animated delta on the one that reads it.
- `EnderDragonModelMixin` / `WitherBossModelMixin` - cancel `setupAnim`; now redundant under `SkipSetupAnimMixin`, kept as model-specific docs.
- `GuardianStateMixin` - for the guardian a posed frame draws, `tailAnimation` and `spikesAnimation` are the clock values the roster carries for the subject's gait (`AnimationClock.clock`); on every other render `tailAnimation = 0`, vanilla seeding it at random; `lookAtPosition` / `lookDirection` = null on every frame.
- `ParrotStandingMixin` (`ParrotRenderer`) - `pose = STANDING` on a posed frame. A never-ticked parrot is off the ground and vanilla draws it flying; the asset side rests a parrot standing in every render, so both posed gaits pin the rest it takes. `setupAnim` is the pose's one reader, so the frozen sub-trees cannot see it.
- `IronGolemOfferMixin` (`IronGolemRenderer`) - on a posed frame `offerFlowerTick` is the golem's own counter less the frame's tick, floored at 0, which is what `aiStep` would have left. The carried trait starts the offer at 400 and nothing ticks the golem, so without it every frame holds one instant of the arm's sway. Written at RETURN, after the renderer resolved the poppy, so the flower stays drawn; a golem offering nothing is untouched.
- `PhantomStateMixin` - `flapTime = getUniqueFlapTickOffset()` taken out, the schedule left in. The renderer builds the field as `offset + ageInTicks`, so the flap is a pure function of the clock and only the id-derived offset has to go; pinning the whole sum took the schedule with it and held the wings still. Inert at tick 0 by construction, so the frozen sub-trees do not see it.
- `PufferfishStateMixin` - `puffState = STATE_FULL`.
- **The `*IdleMixin` family** - `ArmadilloIdleMixin`, `AxolotlIdleMixin`, `BatIdleMixin`, `BreezeIdleMixin`, `CamelIdleMixin`, `CopperGolemIdleMixin`, `CreakingIdleMixin`, `DolphinIdleMixin`, `EnderDragonIdleMixin`, `FrogIdleMixin`, `RabbitIdleMixin`, `ShulkerIdleMixin`, `SnifferIdleMixin`, `SquidIdleMixin`, `WardenIdleMixin`. Not pins: they DRIVE a field vanilla's own `tick` or `aiStep` fills and a never-ticked subject leaves at zero, so that the reference shows the animation rather than a subject frozen at a value no player ever sees. Two shapes, both read from what the asset side decides. A **clock**, whose value at each frame the roster carries and the mixin writes through `AnimationClock.clock` - the harness evaluates no wave, and `StyleCatalogMirrorTest` in asset-renderer's suite holds that every clock the shipped catalog drives has a write here and every write a clock. Each clock write asks `AnimationClock.isSubject(entity)` first, so only the entity a posed frame draws takes its schedule's clocks and a mob of the same kind drawn in the level behind the capture keeps vanilla's value rather than reading a clock that schedule does not carry; `EntityAnimationScheduleTest` in asset-renderer's suite holds the question to every clock and walk read. A **one-hot**, whose selected member answers one, declared on both sides in `IdleFigures` and mirrored as text by the same test; a value that moved on one side only renders happily and reports as a defect in that renderer. Adding a clock is a `CLOCKS` row on the tooling side and a one-line write here; adding a one-hot is a roster line on both sides. All fifteen stay gated on `PoseState.posed()` - a clock write through `isSubject`, which answers false on every frozen frame - so every frozen sub-tree is byte-identical and the two posed ones move.
- `ZombieVillagerStateMixin` - `villagerData = default` (PLAINS/NONE/1).
- `DonkeyModelMixin` / `LlamaModelMixin` - hide equipment-driven `left_chest`/`right_chest` bones.
- `ArmorStandSpawnFlagsMixin` (`ArmorStandModel.<init>`) - force `left_arm` / `right_arm` invisible, reached through `root` because they are declared on `HumanoidModel`. Gives the stand the arms a freshly spawned one has. **Both flags this model reads live in one synched byte that defaults to zero, with opposite senses**: `showArms()` is `(flags & 4) != 0` (plain bit, spawned = no arms) while `showBasePlate()` is `(flags & 8) == 0` (inverted - the stored bit means "no base plate", spawned = plate kept). `SkipSetupAnimMixin` cancels the `setupAnim` that applies both, so every part keeps `ModelPart`'s constructed `visible = true` - right for the plate, wrong for the arms, so only the arms are pinned.
- `TurtleEggBellyMixin` (`AdultTurtleModel.<init>`) - force `eggBelly.visible = false` (asset-renderer toggles the `egg_belly` bone off; a default turtle has `hasEgg = false`). Pinned at model construction for the same reason as `ArmorStandSpawnFlagsMixin` - `setupAnim` (which sets `eggBelly.visible = hasEgg`) is cancelled.
- `ArmadilloShellMixin` (`ArmadilloModel.<init>`) - force `cube.visible = false`. An armadillo has two whole bodies in one mesh and `setupAnim` picks between them off `isHidingInShell`, an uninitialised boolean, so a fresh one is walking. With the skip in force both bodies drew and the shell closed the gap between the legs, disagreeing with the Java render on 5916 pixels of silhouette. **Its constructor is `(ModelPart, AnimationDefinition x4)`** - a `(ModelPart)` injector crashes the client with `InvalidInjectionException` rather than failing to apply.
- `CamelSaddleReinsMixin` (`CamelSaddleModel.<init>`) / `EquineSaddleLinesMixin` (`EquineSaddleModel.<init>`) - force the reins invisible on a saddle nothing is riding, which is what `setupAnim` writes from `isRidden` and the skip leaves at `true`. One class serves five equines: donkey and mule bake their saddle through `DonkeyModel.createSaddleLayer` and are still handed `EquineSaddleModel` to pose it, so the model a layer holds is what says which bones toggle and the factory that baked the mesh is not.

## When to delete a freeze mixin

> **A `setupAnim` freeze is never deleted; it is gated.** `SkipSetupAnimMixin` and the two per-model
> cancels beside it (`EnderDragonModelMixin`, `WitherBossModelMixin` - the dragon's is load-bearing,
> its renderer not being a `LivingEntityRenderer`) are what `entities/` is ground truth FOR, and the
> asset-renderer's own default draws the mesh as authored. Deleting one would move 88 of the 90
> modelled entities. So each of them, and `FreezeAnimationStateMixin`'s `ageInTicks`, reads
> `PoseState.posed()` and the frozen and posed reference sets exist side by side.
>
> A **pin** is a different thing and outlives every freeze: `GlintTexturingMixin`, `WitchNoseMixin`,
> the guardian's tail and gaze, the phantom's flap offset all take an input that varies with the
> client's own history out of the render, and an animated run needs them more than a frozen one does,
> because a frozen run reads most of them through no code at all.
>

> **A pin that reaches nothing under the freeze is not free.** It is unread on every frozen sub-tree
> and read on the two posed ones, so a value chosen to look right is a state the asset side has no
> way to know about and reads as its own defect. A field the asset side drives is written from the
> roster's clock instead - the guardian's spikes stand where vanilla settles a still guardian's in
> `idle/` and a moving one's in `walk/`, values both sides read from one table. Where such a field is
> deterministic already and nothing drives it, leave it alone and say so.
